The real contribution
A record is a footnote. The lasting achievement is a new niche: one where every person, credentialed or not, has the standing to protect their intellectual rights.
This page is deliberately not about a record. A record is just a milestone. The work that matters is defining, classifying and defending the intellectual rights of people who have historically been invisible to institutions.
