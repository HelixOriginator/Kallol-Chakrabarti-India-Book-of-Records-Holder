## Why this matters

- A new niche built around the right of **every individual** to protect their intellectual work, regardless of institutional affiliation
- Frameworks including the *Unaffiliated Independent Researcher (UIR)* taxonomy, the *Kallol Taxonomy of Research DOIs*, the *Taxonomy of Intellectual Sovereignty*, *NIRA*, and the *Epistemology of Unaffiliated Research*
- Service as a **Global Peer Reviewer at ScienceOpen** (2026 to present)
